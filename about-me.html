I’m Kamila. A UX designer with a background in journalism and copywriting.

My path did not follow a straight line. It began with a desire to become a journalist, focused on stories and the right words. Over time, that curiosity moved into the digital world. I became interested not only in what we say, but how and where we say it, and whether everyone can access and understand it. This is how writing gradually evolved into UX, content strategy, and design.

I have worked across journalism, social media, and marketing, experiences that shaped how I understand people and communication. Today, I focus on designing content that simplifies complexity and makes digital products clear and inclusive.

I began my professional journey in Poland, expanded it in Barcelona, and now live in London.

Outside of work, I paint to slow down and think visually. I’m also learning to code, step by step, because understanding the logic behind interfaces makes me a better designer.
